# MASCA committee access and delegation

Approved user specification: one CMS with private National/state workspaces, individual MASCA Google accounts, memberships by committee + area + role, bounded state delegation, record sharing, separate platform/content authority, local independent approval, and term expiry with 30/7-day reminders.

National appoints access managers. Managers may assign Viewer, Editor and Approver within their committee but cannot appoint managers or grant access elsewhere. Members may belong to several committees; removing one membership leaves others intact. Only platform administrators suspend whole accounts.

Each working record belongs to a committee. Drafts, discussions, revisions, assets and presence are private unless shared. Published website content remains public. Record collaborators can view/comment or edit, never approve/publish/delete or administer access solely through sharing. Mentions only notify people already able to access that record.

Authors/contributors cannot approve their own work, and substantive edits invalidate approval. Calendar posting remains manual. Expired memberships stop access on subsequent requests, preserving history.

Roll out through an explicit server feature flag after reviewing migrated grants and supplying actual term end dates; no expiry dates are invented. Existing National workflows continue before activation. Migration must report broad legacy access for National review.
